November 13–14, 2026
Corwin Pavilion · University of California, Santa Barbara Location & directions →
View Photos View Schedule

This conference marked the launch of the Center for Statistical Foundations of AI (CSFAI) at UCSB, which aims to advance principled, interpretable, and trustworthy AI through rigorous statistical methodology. The meeting brought together leading researchers across statistics, machine learning, and related disciplines to discuss emerging challenges and opportunities at the interface of theory, methodology, and applications.

Thank you to everyone who joined us at CSFAI 2026. View photos from the conference.

The program, abstract booklet, and conference room map remain available on the program page.

We will have a special issue of the Journal of Data Science on Statistical Foundations of AI for invited speakers and poster presenters. More details will be announced.
