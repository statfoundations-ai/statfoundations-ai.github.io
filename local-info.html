Venue

Sessions will be held in Corwin Pavilion, located inside the University Center (UCen) at UC Santa Barbara. The pavilion sits next to the UCSB Lagoon and Campus Point Beach, with dining options inside the UCen and a short walk to the cafes and restaurants of Isla Vista. Day parking is available in the lots adjacent to the UCen; permits can be purchased from on-site machines or via the UCSB Transportation & Parking Services portal.

Getting here

UC Santa Barbara is in Goleta, about 10 miles west of downtown Santa Barbara. The closest airport is Santa Barbara Airport (SBA); Los Angeles International (LAX) and Hollywood Burbank (BUR) are common alternatives.

  • From SBA: short trip to campus by taxi or rideshare.
  • From LAX: the Santa Barbara Airbus express bus and the Amtrak Pacific Surfliner train both connect to Goleta or Santa Barbara. Rideshare and rental cars are also options.
  • From BUR: rideshare, rental car, or transfer to Amtrak.

Local transit

Santa Barbara MTD serves the UCSB campus. Line 24x is an express from downtown Santa Barbara, and Line 11 also stops near the UCen.

Local Attractions

Discover Historic Santa Barbara Landmarks

Experience the rich history and striking architecture that defines the American Riviera. Attendees can step back in time at the Old Mission Santa Barbara, a breathtaking 1786 landmark featuring beautiful historic gardens, a museum, and panoramic ocean views. Downtown, the Santa Barbara County Courthouse stands as a stunning 1929 Spanish-Colonial Revival masterpiece; visitors can tour its vibrant mural room and climb the clock tower for an unmatched 360-degree view of the city. For a classic coastal experience, walk out onto Stearns Wharf, California’s oldest continuously operating wooden wharf, packed with local seafood spots, artisan shops, and views of the harbor.

Explore Marine Life in the Santa Barbara Channel

Discover one of the world’s best marine mammal watching destinations right off the coast. Depending on the season, attendees can view majestic blue whales, humpbacks, and gray whales alongside massive pods of dolphins. Local excursions depart directly from the Santa Barbara Harbor. For year-round daily excursions on a large, high-capacity catamaran, check out Condor Express. If you prefer intimate or custom marine tours, explore options with Santa Barbara Whale Watch or enjoy a sleek catamaran sail with the Santa Barbara Sailing Center.

Sip and Savor Santa Barbara Wine Country

Santa Barbara is world-renowned for its distinct microclimates producing exceptional Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, and Syrah. For an easy post-conference activity, visitors can walk the Urban Wine Trail, which connects dozens of boutique tasting rooms across downtown and the trendy Funk Zone district. To explore the scenic vineyards of the nearby Santa Ynez Valley, look into fully hosted day trips and shuttle services provided by Santa Barbara Wine Country Tours or Stagecoach Co. Wine Tours.
